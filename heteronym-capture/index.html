HETERONYM / face capture

every angle of the baseline — recorded once, locally, given to no one by default

This records one continuous guided take (~80 seconds) and pulls still frames the whole way through. You end up with the raw video plus a curated set of sharp frames covering every angle of your face — the reference material for the baseline “mes.”

  • Face a window or soft light source even, diffuse light on the whole face — no hard side shadows, no backlight
  • Plain, uncluttered background a blank wall beats a busy room
  • Camera at eye level, face filling ~⅔ of frame prop the laptop up if you need to
  • Hair off the face, glasses off do a second take with glasses/other hair later if that’s also “you”
  • Neutral-ish makeup or none this take is the baseline; the personas get built on top
  • Move slowly during the turns slow movement = sharp frames at every angle
